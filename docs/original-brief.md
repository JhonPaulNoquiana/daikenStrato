CORE CONCEPT — "FROM THE AIRCON TO THE ROOM"
==================================================

The entire website is one continuous cinematic scroll experience.

At the beginning, the screen is almost completely filled by the air-conditioning unit.

The camera starts extremely close to the AC.

The user can see:
- the texture of the white casing
- subtle reflections
- the vents
- the air blades
- tiny manufacturing details
- realistic material imperfections
- cool air flowing from the vents

The AC should feel physically present in 3D.

As the user scrolls downward, DO NOT simply zoom out a flat image.

Instead, make the camera physically travel backward through a reconstructed 3D environment.

The AC remains anchored in the actual architecture.

The wall becomes visible.

Then the ceiling.

Then the windows.

Then the furniture.

Then the living room.

Then the dining area.

Eventually the entire open-plan interior is revealed.

The final view should feel like the camera has traveled several meters backward through a real luxury home.

==================================================
SCROLL = CAMERA
==================================================

Scrolling should control the camera like a cinematic dolly shot.

SCROLL POSITION 0%
Extreme macro shot of the AC.

SCROLL POSITION 10%
Camera begins pulling away.

SCROLL POSITION 20%
AC vents and surrounding wall become visible.

SCROLL POSITION 35%
Camera reveals the ceiling, windows and architectural details.

SCROLL POSITION 50%
Living room begins appearing.

SCROLL POSITION 65%
Sofa, coffee table, plants and interior details become visible.

SCROLL POSITION 80%
Camera reveals the relationship between living room and dining area.

SCROLL POSITION 100%
Wide cinematic shot of the complete interior.

The transition must be continuous.

No cuts.

No sudden changes.

No obvious 2D zoom effect.

It should feel like one uninterrupted camera movement.
